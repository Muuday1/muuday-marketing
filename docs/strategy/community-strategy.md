# Community Strategy — Brasil Global

## Vision
Build the largest, most valuable online community for Brazilians living abroad — where information flows freely, connections are real, and no one feels alone.

## Platform Strategy

### Primary: Instagram
- Main discovery channel
- Content → DM → Community
- Stories for daily engagement

### Secondary: Discord / WhatsApp
- Private community groups
- Real-time support
- Events coordination

### Tertiary: Website / Blog
- SEO content (evergreen)
- Resource library
- Newsletter signup

### Podcast: Spotify + Apple
- Deep engagement
- Commuter audience
- Expert interviews

## Community Stages

### Stage 1: Content → Audience (Months 1-3)
- Publish high-quality content daily
- Engage with every comment
- DM new followers personally
- Goal: 1,000 engaged followers

### Stage 2: Audience → Community (Months 4-6)
- Launch Discord/WhatsApp group
- Weekly Q&A sessions
- User-generated content campaigns
- Goal: 100 active community members

### Stage 3: Community → Movement (Months 7-12)
- Offline meetups (major cities)
- Community ambassadors program
- Partnerships with brands serving Brazilians abroad
- Goal: 1,000 active members, 10 ambassadors

### Stage 4: Movement → Ecosystem (Year 2+)
- Marketplace for Brazilian services abroad
- Job board
- Mentorship program
- Premium subscriptions

## Engagement Tactics

### Daily
- Reply to all comments within 2 hours
- 3-5 meaningful comments on other accounts
- Story polls, quizzes, Q&A

### Weekly
- Carousel post (educational)
- Reel (entertaining/inspiring)
- Newsletter
- Community spotlight (member story)

### Monthly
- Deep-dive blog article
- Podcast episode with guest
- Community event (online)
- Analytics review + strategy adjustment

## Member Journey

```
Discovery (Instagram post)
    ↓
Engagement (Like/comment/follow)
    ↓
Connection (DM or story reply)
    ↓
Value (Download guide, read article)
    ↓
Community (Join Discord/WhatsApp)
    ↓
Contribution (Share story, help others)
    ↓
Ambassador (Invite others, create content)
```

## Content from Community

### User-Generated Content Campaigns
1. **#MinhaHistóriaNoExterior** — Members share their immigration story
2. **#DicaDoDia** — Members share one practical tip
3. **#SaudadeDeCasa** — Members share what they miss most

### Community Spotlights
- Weekly feature of one community member
- Interview format (text or podcast)
- Builds social proof and belonging
