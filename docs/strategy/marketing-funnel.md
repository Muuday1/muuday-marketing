| Organic social | 40% | Consistent posting, hashtags, engagement  |
| SEO            | 25% | Blog articles, keyword optimization       |

| Referral       | 10% | Partnerships, guest posts, collaborations |
| Direct         | 5%  | Brand recognition, repeat visitors        |

## Meta Ads Strategy

### Campaign Structure

- Account: Muuday
- Campaign: Awareness (Reach) — Ad Set: Lookalike audiences
- Campaign: Consideration (Traffic) — Ad Set: Interest-based
- Campaign: Conversion (Leads) — Ad Set: Retargeting
- Campaign: Retention (Engagement) — Ad Set: Community members

### Audience Targeting

**Cold Audiences**

- Interests: Brazil, immigration, living abroad
- Behaviors: Frequent travelers, expats
- Languages: Portuguese

**Warm Audiences**

- Website visitors (last 30 days)
- Video viewers (50%+)
- Instagram engagers (last 90 days)

**Hot Audiences**

- Newsletter subscribers
- Guide downloaders
- Community members

## Key Metrics by Funnel Stage

### Awareness

- CPM (Cost per 1,000 impressions)
- Reach
- Video view rate
- Follower growth

### Consideration

- CTR (Click-through rate)
- CPC (Cost per click)
- Time on site
- Pages per session

### Conversion

- CPA (Cost per acquisition)
- Conversion rate
- Lead quality score
- Email open rate

### Retention

- Community engagement rate
- Newsletter open rate
- Podcast retention rate
- NPS (Net Promoter Score)
