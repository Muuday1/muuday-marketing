# Content Strategy — Brasil Global

## Brand Positioning
**"O guia definitivo para brasileiros que conquistam o mundo sem perder sua essência."**

Não somos um portal de notícias genérico. Somos a voz da experiência vivida — com dados, empatia, e visão de futuro.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Immigration & Legal (20%)
- Visas, cidadania, regularização
- Documentos, apostilas, traduções
- Direitos do imigrante
- **Tone**: Authoritative, calm, actionable

### 2. Career Abroad (20%)
- Job search, LinkedIn optimization
- Networking cultural (como se networka em cada país)
- Certificações, reconhecimento de diploma
- **Tone**: Motivational, practical, honest

### 3. Finance (15%)
- Banking, credit score, mortgages
- Taxes (double taxation, treaties)
- Investments, crypto, remittances
- **Tone**: Clear, warning-friendly, empowering

### 4. Culture & Identity (20%)
- Saudade, food, traditions
- Dating, relationships, family abroad
- Raising Brazilian kids abroad
- **Tone**: Warm, nostalgic, inclusive

### 5. Community (15%)
- Events, meetups, online groups
- Success stories, interviews
- Mental health, therapy abroad
- **Tone**: Supportive, celebratory, real

### 6. Lifestyle (10%)
- Travel, food, wellness
- Sports, hobbies, entertainment
- **Tone**: Fun, aspirational, accessible

## Quality Rubric (8+/10 to publish)

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Cultural accuracy | 25% | Fact-checked by Brazilian abroad |
| Visual quality | 25% | Professional design, on-brand |
| Copy quality | 20% | Engaging, typo-free, on-voice |
| Value delivered | 20% | Reader learns something actionable |
| Shareability | 10% | Would someone share this? |

## Content Formats

### Instagram Carousel
- 5-10 slides
- First slide: hook + bold visual
- Last slide: CTA + branding
- Font: Inter or Geist (clean, modern)

### Instagram Reel
- 15-60 seconds
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Captions always on (80% watch muted)
- Trending audio when relevant

### Blog Article
- 800-2000 words
- H2/H3 structure
- Internal links to related content
- CTA at end (newsletter, community, etc.)

### Podcast Episode
- 20-40 minutes
- Script generated by AI, reviewed by human
- Voice by ElevenLabs (Brazilian Portuguese)
- Intro music + outro branding
