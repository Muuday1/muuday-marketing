# Strategy Layer

> The brain that decides WHAT to create, WHEN to publish, and WHO to target.

## Modules

### `campaigns/`
- Campaign brief generator (objective, audience, budget, timeline)
- Campaign performance tracker
- Auto-budget reallocation based on ROAS

### `funnels/`
- Funnel designer (awareness → consideration → conversion → retention)
- Funnel performance analytics
- Bottleneck identification

### `briefs/`
- Content brief generator (topic, angle, platform, format, deadline)
- Brief approval workflow
- Brief-to-content pipeline

## Workflow

```
Intelligence Layer (trends, competitors, audience)
         ↓
Strategy Layer (decides what to create)
         ↓
Content Engine (creates the content)
         ↓
Canvas (composes with brand)
         ↓
Publisher (distributes)
         ↓
Analytics (measures)
         ↓
Strategy Layer (adjusts)
```
