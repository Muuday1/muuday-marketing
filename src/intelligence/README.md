# Intelligence Layer

> The eyes and ears of the marketing machine. Knows what's happening before anyone else.

## Modules

### `trends/`
- Scrape Google Trends, TikTok Trends, X Trends
- Identify trending topics relevant to Brazilians abroad
- Alert when a trend matches our content pillars
- Score trend relevance and urgency

### `competitors/`
- Monitor competitor Instagram + LinkedIn accounts
- Track their posting frequency, engagement, content themes
- Identify content gaps (what they're NOT covering)
- Alert when competitor launches something new

### `audience/`
- Build persona profiles from analytics data
- Segment audience by country, age, interest
- Track sentiment from comments and DMs
- Identify micro-influencers in the community

## Outputs

All intelligence feeds into the **Strategy Layer**:
- Daily trend brief → Content Engine
- Weekly competitor report → Strategy review
- Monthly audience deep-dive → Campaign planning
